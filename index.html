BudgetMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy applies to the BudgetMate Android app (“BudgetMate”), provided by the publisher identified on its Google Play listing.

Information stored in the app

BudgetMate stores information you enter so it can provide its budgeting features. This may include transaction amounts, dates, types and categories; subscription names, prices, billing dates and reminder settings; and contact names, phone numbers, email addresses, notes, and records of amounts owed or shared. It also stores app preferences, such as display, onboarding and notification settings. BudgetMate does not read your address book; contact details are those you enter into the app.

BudgetMate does not require an account. The app does not request internet access and does not include BudgetMate-operated analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. BudgetMate does not send your financial records to a BudgetMate server.

How information is used

The information is used on your device to display and organize your finances, calculate balances, manage subscriptions, and provide reminders you enable.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, BudgetMate schedules reminders on your device, including daily reminders and subscription reminders. Android controls whether notifications can be shown. Notification text may be visible on your lock screen depending on your Android settings. You can change reminder preferences in BudgetMate and notification access in Android settings.

Backups and information shared with other services

BudgetMate itself does not transmit the information you enter to its publisher or a BudgetMate server. Android backup and device-transfer rules include BudgetMate’s local database and preferences. If backup or transfer is enabled on your device, Android’s configured backup service may copy this information under that service’s terms and your device/account settings.

CSV exports are saved to your device’s Downloads folder. When importing, you choose a file through Android’s file picker. You may also choose other apps or storage providers when managing exported files; those services handle the files under their own terms. Files you export can remain on your device after app data is deleted.

Security

App records are stored in BudgetMate’s private app storage and are protected by Android’s app isolation and your device’s security settings. BudgetMate does not add separate encryption to these records. Copies placed in device backups or exported files are subject to the protections and settings of the backup or file-storage service you use. Keep your device secured and manage backups and exported files carefully.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain in BudgetMate until you delete them, clear BudgetMate’s app data, or uninstall the app. You can delete records from within the app where that feature is provided. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes the app’s local copy, but does not necessarily delete copies already included in device backups or CSV files. Manage those copies through your device, backup account, or file-storage service.

Children’s privacy

BudgetMate is a general personal-finance utility and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes to this policy

This page will show the current policy. If BudgetMate’s data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe them.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests about BudgetMate, email budgetmate.privacy@gmail.com.
